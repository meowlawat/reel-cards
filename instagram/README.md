# Reel Cards inside Reels

Open index.html or visit the /instagram/ edition of the live demo. This independent Instagram interface study uses three prepared photo lessons. Swipe vertically, use the arrow keys, or tap Next to change reels. Pause the timed steps if you want to read one for longer.

## How is a Reel Card different from Save?

**Save** bookmarks the original reel in Saved. Open it when you want to watch the whole lesson again. A bookmark never enters the review queue.

**Save as Reel Card** keeps a question and answer about the lesson in Learned. Edit the question to capture what *you* forget. Try answering, flip, and rate your recall. The first successful review returns tomorrow, the second after three days, and the third after eight. A forgotten card returns tomorrow.

For example, save the pasta reel to watch the cook's sequence. Choose Reel Card to practise remembering which ingredient makes the sauce silky. You can choose either or both. The two collections persist separately in this browser.
